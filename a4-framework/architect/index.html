Architect: Designing the AI Operating Model and Governance

Architect is where you design the company that AI is going to run through. Chapter 4 calls it the architecture problem nobody discusses: silos block the flow of information, hierarchies slow decisions, and specialists rarely connect their work. You design three layers together (organizational structure, data architecture and process architecture), put AI governance around them, and finish with your AI-First Blueprint after three to four months. The book’s rule for this stage is short: “Do not automate bad processes.” Tools 15 to 18 cover the roadmap, the blueprint, a 54-item governance checklist and Human-AI Symbiosis role mapping.
