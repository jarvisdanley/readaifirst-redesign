AI workshops that get your leadership team on one plan

Working sessions built on the A4 Framework and the leadership session tools from my book. Your team walks in with a stack of pilots and walks out agreed on what comes first.

Start with a 20-minute call
Placeholdersession photo. Alt text: Jeff Danley facilitating an A4 session with a leadership team

A common pattern in leadership teams is that everyone agrees AI matters and each leader carries a different list of what comes first. Every list has merit, and together they stall the budget. These sessions turn those lists into one plan, built in the room by the people who’ll own it, using the same tools I wrote into AI-First. Whatever the format, the outcome is agreement, written up in a readout your team can act on.

Who it fits

  • You have AI pilots in several departments and no single plan that ties them together
  • You’re setting a budget and want the leadership team to own the priorities
  • A new executive needs the team aligned quickly

Pick the depth your team needs

A4 Leadership Session

Two hours, with an optional extension to a half day. On site or virtual.

Who attends
The CEO and six to ten members of the executive team.
The session
The session my book asks every leadership team to hold, run with Tools 05 and 06. We look honestly at where the company stands, talk through what’s at stake, and settle one question: are we ready to commit to the Assess stage?
What comes out
A recorded decision on launching the assessment, a named sponsor and assessment lead, and a one-page readout of what the team agreed. With the extension, you also get a first position on the AI Adoption Spectrum and a shortlist of opportunities.

A4 Assess: Leadership Alignment Session

Half day. On site or virtual.

Who attends
8 to 12 leaders from one company who own AI budget, operations and technology decisions.
The session
For leadership teams with pilots running and no shared plan. Before the session each leader completes the Mirror Test and the AI-First Self-Assessment, so the room’s time goes to decisions.
What comes out
A readiness readout across the Four Foundations, your top three AI use cases ranked with the Value-Feasibility Matrix and Strategic Selection, and a first owner and measure for each, all captured in a readout in your inbox within five business days.
