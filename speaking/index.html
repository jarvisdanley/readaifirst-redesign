Speaking

Keynotes for the leaders turning AI pilots into enterprise systems

Talks built on the A4 Framework from my book, AI-First. Your audience leaves with something they can put on the table at Monday’s leadership meeting.

Check a dateDownload the speaker kit

The pattern I talk about most is an enterprise with more AI pilots than anyone can count and only a handful running in daily work.

  • The stretch between the demo and the workflow is where the advantage gets built.

  • My talks cover how the companies getting this right pick what to fund, how they build AI into the way decisions get made, and how they grow it into the Central Nervous System of the business.

  • I’ve spent 25 years building technology people said nobody would use, including a co-invention on US Patent 7,376,091, part of the foundation of today’s mobile hotspot. Today I lead enterprise AI strategy and run a portfolio of AI agents, so I bring an operator’s view to the stage.

Keynote topics

Three talks, one system

Each talk follows a stage of the A4 Framework and lands on the decision your audience faces at that point in its budget year. Every talk runs as a keynote (45 to 60 minutes), a working session (90 minutes) or a short session for an executive team.

Plenty of enterprises run dozens of AI pilots, and far fewer have connected them. I lay out the A4 Framework and show how the companies pulling ahead build on the Four Foundations of AI Readiness and the Three Layers of AI-First Architecture.

Takeaways

  • A fast read on your organization against the Four Foundations
  • The architecture layer that most often holds a promising pilot back
  • Where to start closing the Widening Gap this year
Activate · Best for January to March kickoffs

The First 90 Days of Your 2027 AI Budget PlaceholderJeff to confirm title

The budget is approved and the clock starts. This talk walks through the 90-Day Activation Sprint: how to pick the use cases worth funding first, who owns each one, and how to measure them so the results hold up at the mid-year review.

Takeaways

  • The Strategic Selection Framework for ranking use cases
  • A week-by-week sprint plan with a named owner for each use case
  • Three-Tier Measurement your CFO will recognize
Activate and Amplify · Best for mid-year conferences and CIO forums

Scale It or Sunset It: Getting Your AI Program Ready for the Mid-Year Review PlaceholderJeff to confirm title

By mid-year every AI program faces one question: what earns more funding, and what gets retired? This talk shows how to make that call with evidence, and how to spot Success Theater before it reaches the board deck.

Takeaways

  • The Adaptive Scaling Framework checkpoints
  • How to spot Success Theater and the Delegation Trap in your own reporting
  • The Four Phases of Cultural Transformation
