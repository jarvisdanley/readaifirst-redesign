The Book

AI-First: Building the Central Nervous System of the Modern Enterprise

The book starts where most AI programs stall, with plenty of activity and very little connecting it. I argue that the companies pulling ahead treat AI as the organization’s Central Nervous System, the way the whole company senses change, turns information into insight, coordinates action and keeps learning. The A4 Framework is how you build that inside a company that already has legacy systems, a budget cycle and an org chart.
